## Brief threshold

The brief states: *"If >1 OVERTURNED, the whole 29-lead batch is suspect."* Only 1 OVERTURNED (c073 Rhett Bray / BeaconPath) — under the threshold, but the 2 FLAGGED leads (c044, c170) plus the CEFEX misrepresentation in c188 indicate the prior verifier did show a pattern of generosity on weaker leads. **Batch is NOT condemned**, but **prior evidence files need targeted corrections** for c044, c073, c170, and c188.

## Systemic patterns observed

1. **Gate 4 is the weaker gate.** Multiple leads (c044, c073, c170, c217) have Gate 4 evidence resting on **single-source firm-marketing language** ("we work with self-funded employers") without named clients, stop-loss/TPA partnerships, or published case studies. The brief explicitly warned against this. The high-signal Gate 4 leads (c001, c005, c012, c017, c026, c188) all had at least one of: named proprietary plan product, named mid-market client logos, or independent press coverage.

2. **Health Rosetta directory listing was over-counted.** Multiple Gate 2 calls relied on `healthrosetta.org/people/<x>/` listings without distinguishing between Certified Advisor, Charter Certified, Associate Advisor, or *directory-only* listing. c073 (Bray) is the clearest example — listed in HR directory but with no designation tier, no firm-site HR language, no fiduciary thought-leadership. Some prior evidence files cited verbatim quotes that I could not reproduce on fresh fetch — possible hallucinated verbatim text (notably the alleged BeaconPath team-page "proud member of Health Rosetta" quote and the FoxWatch "fiduciaries their clients need" quote).

3. **Credential conflation.** c188 (Procida) — prior file conflated retirement-plan CEFEX (a real cert held by their RIA affiliate, *Innovative Investment Fiduciaries*) with a health-plan fiduciary signal. The genuine health-plan Gate 2 signal is the CAA-2021 medical-fiduciary content suite, which is independently strong. Net verdict unchanged but the evidence framing was misleading.

4. **Affiliate/alliance signals are real but should not be the sole signal.** Mitigate Partners affiliation appeared as a "self-funded book" signal for c005, c170 — fine when stacked with concrete firm-specific evidence, but not sufficient alone.
